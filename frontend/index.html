System Architecture

Designed for unhesitating clarity.

Standardized wayfinding and risk modeling replace ambiguous reports with strict topological guidance.

01 • SIGNAL TAXONOMY

Deterministic Hazard Classification

Every hazard—from surface defects to severe collisions—is evaluated against a strict 1-to-10 severity scale and radius of influence, eliminating cognitive clutter for pedestrians and drivers.

Classification Mode Severity & Hazard Taxonomy
02 • MULTI-OBJECTIVE ENGINE

Multi-Objective Safe Routing

Calculate three mathematically distinct routes between any coordinates: the fastest path for urgent transit, the risk-minimized safest path, and a balanced optimal trajectory.

Engine Output Fastest • Safest • Optimal Paths
03 • COLLECTIVE VERIFICATION

Citizen Consensus Ledger

Community members report incidents with precision map-point pickers. Real-time consensus voting confirms active status and filters out outdated or false alerts.

Verification Type Community Validated Consensus
Operational Workflow

How Street Safety System Works

A seamless cycle connecting civic incident reporting with algorithmic safe wayfinding across municipal networks.

01

Report or Detect

Citizens and transit sensors flag road defects, collisions, or infrastructure failures with precise geographic coordinates and hazard classification.

02

Consensus Verification

Nearby travelers verify alerts in real-time, building a tamper-evident reputation score for every street condition.

03

Safe Navigation

Routing algorithms factor in hazard severity and proximity radii, steering pedestrians and drivers onto safety-optimized paths.
